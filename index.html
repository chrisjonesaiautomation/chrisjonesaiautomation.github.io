About

From data analyst to AI builder

I started out as a data analyst, working with tools like Python, SQL, and Tableau to turn raw data into decisions. Over the past few months I've gone all-in on AI automation — learning to build real, working agents with Claude and modern AI tooling instead of just reading about them.

I'm based in Mt Prospect, IL, and I'm currently building out an automation practice from the ground up: shipping real projects first, then bringing that hands-on experience to small businesses that need practical automation, not hype.
